# Human review language

Use this wording, or a close variant, whenever a skill affects students.

- AI drafts. The human decides.
- The artifact is a first pass, not a final decision.
- Low-confidence items go into a review queue, not into routine output silently.
- Keep the evidence, AI draft, human edits, and final decision together.
- No grade, message, sanction, recommendation, or student-facing page is released until a human reads it.
