# Your first task, no installation

Start with a draft announcement. It needs no student records and gives you something useful to review.

1. Open the [visual guide](web/dist/index.html) from your downloaded toolkit and choose **Draft a class update**. Or open [Announcement writer](skills/announcement-writer/SKILL.md) directly.
2. Copy the drafting prompt. Open your institution-approved AI chat and paste it there.
3. Replace the bracketed placeholders with your course, facts, desired length, and a short description of your tone. If you have a past announcement, include one with private details removed. A full voice guide is optional for your first draft.
4. Read the result. Check every date, time zone, link, point value, and policy against your real course materials.
5. Edit it yourself and post it when ready. The AI does not send it for you.

**Success looks like:** one draft you can use after review, with no invented deadline or policy. If the AI needs a missing fact, supply it or keep a visible placeholder.

## Not sure which task fits?

Copy [the Start Here prompt](skills/start-here/SKILL.md). Your AI will ask only the unanswered questions needed to choose a task. You do not need to ingest your whole course first.

## Want to reuse the method?

Save the prompt, or [install the skill](INSTALL.md) if your agent supports skills. Both use the same workflow. If your tool cannot open repository links, paste the actual text or attach the file.

## Want help gathering materials?

Check whether your agent has an authorized connection to the relevant LMS or files. Start with one course and one item. If it cannot read the source, provide a small approved export. The [agent guide](guides/agents.md) explains how retrieval, missing coverage, and human review work.

For grading tasks, use pseudonymous student IDs and a published rubric. For a first demonstration, use an invented response. Never use a style detector to decide whether a student cheated.
